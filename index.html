Taiwan returns bottles.
It rarely refills them.

We checked 10 companies selling skin care in Taiwan. One refill price is confirmed on an official site. Most programs are media-reported.

See the comparison

Research date 2026-10-30. Scope: since 2023. Method: web search by one agent per company.

What we found

No company in this set offers a true package-free skincare range at scale in Taiwan.

The market is dominated by empty-bottle return programs run by imported brands. Real refill products are rare.

Refills are confirmed for two brands. Sabon sells a 500ml shower-oil refill at NT$1,180 versus NT$1,380 for the bottle, about 14% cheaper. Elixir has had refill packs since April 2021 (media only). L'Occitane refill pouches are reported, but their price and terms are unconfirmed.

Bottle returns reward customers modestly. L'Occitane gives NT$10 off per bottle (cap NT$200). The Body Shop gave NT$10 off per bottle (cap NT$100, 2021 campaign). Kiehl's gives 10 member points per bottle.

LUSH is the only naked, package-free model in the set, but its Taiwan return terms could not be read from the official site.

Local brands are weaker on structured programs. Greenvines reports a bottle-recycling program since 2018 (500,000+ bottles by Dec 2024). YUAN has none, only recycled-plastic bottles (reported) and FSC paper.

picupi and Zero Living are real refill and bulk retailers, but their refills are household cleaners, not skin care.

Evidence quality is the main limitation. Most official Taiwan sites returned errors (403 or an expired certificate), so most claims are media-reported.

Only one refill has a verified discount

Sabon's official Taiwan site (fetched 2026-10-30) lists the 500ml shower oil at NT$1,380 and the 500ml refill pack at NT$1,180, about 14% cheaper. The bars are drawn to scale.

Elixir's 2021 refills cost NT$900–1,000, but no new-bottle price was found for comparison. L'Occitane's shampoo refill showed about NT$832 at a retailer, with no confirmed official price.

Takeaways from the comparison

  • Only Sabon has a verified refill price advantage. Others lack a published refill-versus-new comparison.
  • Return programs are largely discount- or points-driven, with no named recycling partner in most cases. Named partners: Story Wear and CCF (Kiehl's campaign), Taiwan Sanhai Angels (Kose, donation recipient), Baodao Jingxiang Tuan (LUSH cosmetics drive).
  • Certifications specific to the Taiwan programs: none found. Greenvines claims B Corp, Cradle to Cradle and 1% for the Planet (its own homepage; the Cradle to Cradle claim had no independent corroboration). YUAN reports GMP, ISO 22716, ISO 9001 and B Corp (one media report).

Company details

LUSH TaiwanUK brand · naked products

UK brand, run as a 100% UK direct operation in Taiwan (an earlier distributor ended in 2015). Sells naked solid products: shampoo and conditioner bars, soap, bath products, fresh face masks.

A "Bring It Back" packaging return exists on the official Taiwan site, but its terms page was unreadable. A cosmetics donation drive with Baodao Jingxiang Tuan ran 2026-03-18 to 2026-03-31 at five stores: Taipei Ximen, Zhongxiao and Xinyi Vieshow, Taichung LaLaport, and Kaohsiung Hanshin Arena.

Unconfirmed: the NT$20 / 5-package reward, the store list, the recycled-content figure.

Kiehl's TaiwanUS brand · L'Oréal Group

Owned by L'Oréal since 2000. Reported: one genuine empty bottle earns 10 member points (TVBS, 2025-07-07). An Earth Month campaign involved Story Wear and CCF. Sources disagree on whether other brands' bottles count. No refill format found.

Unconfirmed: whether the program is permanent.

L'Occitane TaiwanFrench brand

Reported by TVBS and Marie Claire: NT$10 off per empty bottle, capped at NT$200 per purchase. A 2025 upgrade turns returned bottles into recycled-PET phone lanyards, a Gold Card birthday gift (one bottle per lanyard, claimed to save 63.4 g carbon and 2.7 L water).

Refill pouches exist, for example a 500ml shampoo refill at about NT$832 on Yahoo Shopping.

Unconfirmed: official terms, store coverage, other-brand acceptance, and recycled-content claims (the 81.5% and 77% figures are unverified).

Kose Sekkisei 雪肌精Japanese brand · Taiwan Kose

Latest confirmed campaign: 1 Jul–31 Aug 2025, a SAVE the BLUE empty-bottle return with a canvas tote and a donation to Taiwan Sanhai Angels (coral restoration). The donation amount was not stated.

Earlier: a Feb 2018 discount-for-bottles event and a 2023 NT$10-per-share donation. No 2026 Taiwan edition found. No refill.

The Body Shop TaiwanUK brand

In-store empty-bottle return with discounts, reported as running since 1988. A 2021 campaign gave NT$10 off per bottle, up to 10 bottles and NT$100 per purchase.

Refill stations exist in Malaysia, the UK and Singapore, but none was found in Taiwan. Its official site failed with an expired certificate.

Unconfirmed: current 2025–2026 terms.

Sabon TaiwanIsraeli brand · in Taiwan since 2014

Official site (fetched 2026-10-30): 500ml shower-oil refill packs in White Tea and SABON Classic at NT$1,180 (bottle NT$1,380). Green Rose and Delicate Jasmine refills were sold out.

Other prices: body scrub 600g NT$2,080, body lotion 150ml NT$1,380. No recycling program, no eco certifications stated, and no solid soap bars on the Taiwan site.

Elixir 怡麗絲爾Japanese brand · Shiseido group

Refill packs for toner and emulsion launched in Taiwan in April 2021, with a claimed ~85% plastic reduction (a brand claim). The Shiseido parent is common knowledge, not stated in the source.

2021 prices: moisturizing water 150ml NT$900; moisturizing emulsion 110ml NT$1,000; brightening water 150ml NT$1,000; brightening emulsion 110ml NT$1,000.

Based on one article (Beauty321, 2021-04-21). Current availability unconfirmed.

Greenvines 綠藤生機Taiwanese clean-skincare brand

The homepage (greenvines.com.tw) states an empty-bottle recycling program launched in 2018 with 500,000+ bottles collected by December 2024, and a Lalaport Taichung North counter opened in August 2025. No refill program found.

Homepage certifications: B Corp (2015, ranking disputed), 1% for the Planet, and Cradle to Cradle (no independent corroboration).

Unconfirmed: mechanism, partner, incentive, stores.

YUAN 阿原Taiwanese herbal-soap brand

Production base in Shimen and a farm on Yangmingshan. No zero-waste, refill or take-back program found. That means nothing was found, not a confirmed absence.

Reported packaging changes: bottle bodies 100% PCR and FSC-certified paper (Yam News, 2026-03-10); clear plastic film dropped in a July 2022 redesign. The official website could not be confirmed.

picupi 挑品 and Zero Living 零居Taiwanese package-free retail

picupi, a Taiwanese cleaning-products brand, runs a Xinyi (Taipei) "net zero living lab" where customers refill laundry detergent, stain remover and dish liquid into their own bottles. On 2026-04-20 it launched five products with FamilyMart, some in recycled milk bottles.

Zero Living is a package-free shop in Banqiao (listed in 2020). It reportedly stocks a shampoo bar and bamboo toothbrushes.

No skin-care refill was confirmed at either. Opening years and current status are unverified.

Gaps and caveats

  • Official sites were mostly unreachable, so only Sabon's refill pricing and Greenvines' program headline are official-site facts.
  • Python was unavailable, so the conversion script and field validator did not run. The report was written by hand from the JSON files, and the agents checked field coverage manually.
  • Uncertain values: the research skill says to drop them. A few key reported figures are kept here, labeled as reported or unconfirmed.
  • Candidates dropped for lack of evidence: Aesop, MUJI, Ethique, Dr. Bronner's, Drunk Elephant.
  • Coverage is not exhaustive. Results are capped at 10 companies. Taiwan-local solid-soap makers and other package-free shops were not researched individually.
